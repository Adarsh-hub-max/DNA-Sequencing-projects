DNA SEQUENCING

Upload Dataset For Testing The machine Learning Model

To evaluate the performance of our DNA sequence classification model, we provide the functionality to upload a custom dataset for testing. The uploaded dataset should be in a CSV format and must follow the structure expected by the trained model, including a column for DNA sequences and their corresponding labels (e.g., Human, Chimpanzee, Dog). Once the dataset is uploaded, it is processed and passed through the Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier, which predicts the species based on the given DNA sequences. This allows users to validate the model’s accuracy and generalization capability on new, unseen data.
